The provisions for consent provided for under Regulation (EU) 2016/679/EU shall apply to natural persons and, mutatis mutandis, to legal persons 1a. Paragraph 1 is without prejudice to national legislation on determining the persons who are authorised to represent a legal person in any dealings with third parties or in legal proceedings.
