The Commission shall be assisted by the Communications Committee established under Article 118 of Directive (EU) 2018/1972. That committee shall be a committee within the meaning of Regulation (EU) No 182/2011[1] Where reference is made to this paragraph, Article 5 of Regulation (EU) No 182/2011 shall apply.
